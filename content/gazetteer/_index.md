Four states, one spine of mountains. The Gazetteer is the wide-angle
lens: an area-by-area census of the climbing between the Rio Grande and
the Missouri headwaters, each entry with its rock, its style, its rough
size, and the guidebook that covers it properly. Route lists live in the
local guides; this is the map of the territory.
