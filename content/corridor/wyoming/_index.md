Wyoming is the corridor's dolomite and limestone heartland, and its
best-documented ground: Lander built a town's identity around Todd
Skinner's Wild Iris, and Ten Sleep Canyon grew from a local secret
into an international destination — and into one of American sport
climbing's hardest ethics fights. Come for the pocketed stone; read
the access history before you clip a bolt.
