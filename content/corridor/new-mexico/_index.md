The corridor's southern gate is high desert: granite domes above the
Rio Grande gorge country, thirty minutes from Taos, where trad
climbers still outnumber the bolts. It is thinner documented online
than the ranges further north — a reminder that this book is a
starting point for fieldwork, not the last word on any of it.
