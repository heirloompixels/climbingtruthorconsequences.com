Colorado holds both ends of this book's rock spectrum: the volcanic
tuff of the San Luis Valley, and — strung along the highest paved
highway in the state — nearly a thousand routes of Sawatch granite at
Independence Pass. Between Penitente's short technical climbs and
Monitor Rock's easy-access slabs, there's a day here for almost any
climber willing to drive a little.
