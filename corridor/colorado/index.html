Colorado

Alpine granite above Aspen, volcanic pockets in the San Luis Valley.

Colorado holds both ends of this book’s rock spectrum: the volcanic tuff of the San Luis Valley, and — strung along the highest paved highway in the state — nearly a thousand routes of Sawatch granite at Independence Pass. Between Penitente’s short technical climbs and Monitor Rock’s easy-access slabs, there’s a day here for almost any climber willing to drive a little.

  1. Independence Pass 5.4–5.14, moderates concentrated at Monitor Rock Nearly a thousand routes of Sawatch granite strung along the highest paved highway in Colorado, between Aspen and Twin Lakes.
  2. Penitente Canyon 5.7–5.13, skewing 5.10+ Bulletproof volcanic tuff in the San Luis Valley, under a painted Virgin — short, fierce, technical, and worth every finger tip.
