The Corridor

Chosen moderate classics of the Rockies, from the Rio Grande to the Missouri.

Between New Mexico and Montana runs a corridor of rock we keep returning to. This part is not exhaustive and does not try to be: for each area we give the story, the logistics, and a hand-picked selection of the moderate routes — mostly 5.8 to 5.10 — that we would drive a day for, with pointers to the full local guidebooks that deserve your money.

  1. New Mexico Granite domes and desert stone at the corridor's southern gate.
  2. Colorado Alpine granite above Aspen, volcanic pockets in the San Luis Valley.
  3. Wyoming Dolomite and limestone heartland — Lander and Ten Sleep.
  4. Montana Limestone gulches and gneiss ridgelines at the corridor's northern end.
