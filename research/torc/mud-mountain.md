# Mud Mountain

A limestone sport crag directly overlooking the city of Truth or
Consequences — the closest developed climbing to town and, on the
evidence, the most recently developed crag in the county. Mountain
Project describes it as "a beautiful winter limestone crag overlooking
the city of Truth or Consequences." All the sport climbs are well
bolted, with user-friendly anchors, and — unusually — **each route has a
plaque with its name and grade below it**.

Four walls are named: Winter Wall, Crow Feather Wall, Yellow Wall and
Vacation Wall. Known grade range is **5.8 to 5.12**.

**Mountain Project:** https://www.mountainproject.com/area/116221880/mud-mountain

Wall-to-wall movement at the crag, which *was* recovered:

- **Winter Wall** — the first wall you reach.
- **Crow Feather Wall** — follow the trail along the base and descend a
  short, easy down-climb.
- **Yellow Wall** — reached by following a trail across the scree.
- **Vacation Wall** — continue up the switchbacks along the Crow Feather
  Wall to the saddle, then follow **cairns** west to a rock point.

## Rock & Style

- **Rock:** limestone.
- **Style:** sport, exclusively so far as the record shows. Well bolted,
  user-friendly anchors.
- **Length:** unknown — no pitch lengths or bolt counts were captured.
  Nothing suggests multi-pitch; the wall-to-wall descriptions read as
  single-pitch cragging.
- **Distinctive feature:** named-and-graded plaques at the base of each
  route.

## Season

**Winter.** This is explicitly a winter crag.

**Winter Wall** — the best wall here — **faces south** and gets sun most
of the day. Shade creeps in from **left to right starting around 2:00pm
in December**. Aspects for Crow Feather, Yellow and Vacation Walls were
not captured, though the Vacation Wall's position west of the saddle
suggests a different exposure.

Summer is presumably out at this elevation in southern New Mexico, but
that is inference, not a sourced statement.

  Winter Wall (`116254445`) are far higher than those for Caballo Lake
