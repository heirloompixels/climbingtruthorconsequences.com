# T-or-C gap-fill pass — 2026-08-21 (orchestrator session)

A second search pass run after the search budget replenished, to
enrich the wave-1 deep dive. Everything below is search-snippet
sourced from the cited pages; grades given are as quoted.

## Mud Mountain — sectors and approach (solid)

Four walls, all single-pitch, well-bolted sport with user-friendly
anchors and name-and-grade plaques below the routes; lots of winter
sun; overall range 5.8–5.12.

| Wall | Routes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Winter Wall (MP 116254445) | 19 | Best wall; 5.8–5.12; faces south, sun most of the day; in December shade creeps left-to-right from ~2pm, much of the left side shaded by 4pm. First wall reached. |
| Crow Feather Wall | 13 | 5.8–5.12; reached from Winter Wall via a short, easy down-climb along the base. |
| Vacation Wall | 7 | Continue up the switchbacks along Crow Feather Wall. |
| Yellow Wall | 2 | Trail across the scree at the bottom of the down-climb. |

Approach: go under the interstate and set the odometer to 0; head up
over the dam; at 1.4 mi turn right into a large arroyo marked by a
cairn; follow tracks 0.5–0.6 mi to a doubletrack on the left up onto
the mesa; follow to the water tank and park (upper parking possible
with high-clearance 4WD); 2.9 mi total. From parking, follow cairns
along the ridge then down across the wash.

Known route pages: Farewell, Bella Luca (MP 116228913), Gambit
(MP 120613110 — "long continuation of the 'Unknown 5.10a' route on
Super Hero Wall… into the large prominent left-facing corner on the
right side of the wall" — NOTE: MP files Gambit under the T-or-C
area; the Super Hero Wall reference suggests it may belong to the Bat
Cave's Grey Wall rather than Mud Mountain — verify).

## Caballo Lake / Bat Cave Area — structure (solid)

Caballo Lake (MP 106527519) → Bat Cave Area (MP 106527523), "the main
developed area above Caballo Lake", overall 5.6–5.13 on mostly
west-facing limestone, single and multi-pitch sport. Sub-areas:

| Sector | MP id | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Bat Cave | 106527528 | Cave starts ~30 ft above the ground, ~100 ft tall, capped by a triangular roof up to 30 ft deep; accessed by a 30-ft ladder from the base of the wall (Climb Abroad, 2017). Hard classics: Helsinki 5.14d, Titanoboa 5.13d (as quoted in search results — verify spelling "Titanaboa" vs "Titanoboa"). |
| Grey Wall (a.k.a. Super Hero Wall) | — | Super Hero (MP 106636759): third route from the left end; 5.10- to the first anchor, 5.11 all the way. Gotham's Finest (MP 106627930). "Unknown 5.10a" + Gambit continuation. |
| Gotham City | — | Beyond the end of the road past High Camp. |
| High Camp | — | Short (70–80 ft) yellow/orange wall directly above the upper parking ("High Camp"). |
| Trojan Wall (Right of Bat Cave) | 110052447 | |
| Tufa Wall | 108003808 | Park at the "T" 1.9 mi up the 4x4 road; walk/drive 0.1 mi east to High Camp; right-branch trail up steps and a rising traverse. |

History color (Climb Abroad, 2017): locals said the area was
developed largely by the owner of an Albuquerque climbing gym, who
bolted most routes to be friendly to beginners and older climbers;
approach described as past farms, through a tunnel under the dam, and
up a twisty dirt road.

## Luna Park (MP 107034899) — logistics and routes (solid)

Mostly moderate sport on quality volcanic rock. Three moderates at
5.7–5.8 on a longer bolted flat face; the rest 5.9–5.11. Longest
routes on the buttress left of the slot. Sub-area: Upper Park
(MP 112818889).

Named routes: Todaluna 5.9; Lunatique 5.10+; Wolfman 5.9 (5.10
direct); Luna Barr (MP 112277995); Mooning the Moonies (MP 107034974);
Man in the Moon (MP 107035061).

Camping: small three-site USFS campground at the crag, no water.
Season: mid-March–May and September–mid-November. Access: FR 225 via
FR 139 from Monticello (south) or from the I-25 west frontage road
~5 mi south of exit 100 (Red Rock).

## Percha Creek, Hillsboro (MP 105789954) — access caution (solid)

Small andesite/basalt crag under the NM 152 bridge a few minutes past
Hillsboro; moderate trad and sport, mixed crack and bolted face,
5-minute approach. Park at the pullout before the bridge (old Percha
Creek bridge sign), hop the rock wall, walk right a few hundred feet;
climbs on the east side of the creek. **Access status unknown —
route pages say local climbers are working to obtain landowner
permission.** A "mini-guide" photo exists on MP (105789958). Routes:
Pepe's School of Beauty (MP 106478364), The Bridge Troll
(MP 107959116), Rolling Stones Gather no Mas (MP 107959082).
