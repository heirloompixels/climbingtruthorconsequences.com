# Luna Park

A volcanic sport crag with **easy camping** — Mountain Project describes
it as "mostly moderate sport climbing on quality volcanic rock." There
is a **Luna Park campground**, reached by Forest Service roads, which
makes this the most weekend-friendly destination found in the county
despite being the furthest from town.

Route names recovered from the area lean hard into a **lunar theme**
(Man in the Moon, Giant Leap, Zero Gravity, Luna Barr, and one route
literally called "Clever Lunar-Themed Route Name"), which suggests a
single developer or a tight crew with a sense of humor.

**Mountain Project:** https://www.mountainproject.com/area/107034899/luna-park

## Location & Approach

North and west of Truth or Consequences, in Forest Service country
toward the Black Range / Gila edge. Luna Park shares its road network
with [Red Rock Arroyo](./red-rock-arroyo.md).

The one approach recovered, from the north:

> From **I-25 Exit 115**, go **east** and follow **road #1** for about
> **11 miles**, then continue **south** and then **west** on **Forest
> Service Road #225** to **Luna Park campground**.

> **Unverified and probably garbled.** "Go east from Exit 115" to reach
> a crag west of the interstate is internally odd — this text was
> recovered from a search summary that was simultaneously describing
> Red Rock Arroyo's approaches, and the two may have been conflated.
> **Do not trust this approach.** Read the area page directly.

**No GPS coordinates were captured.** Road surface, vehicle
requirements, and approach time from the campground are all unknown.

## Season

**No seasonal or aspect information was captured.**

Luna Park sits well above the Rio Grande valley in Forest Service
terrain, so it very likely runs **spring through fall** — the inverse of
the winter limestone at Mud Mountain and Caballo Lake, and potentially
the answer to "where do I climb around TorC in summer." **This is
inference and needs confirming**, but if true it is an important
structural point for the guidebook.

## Access

- Reached by **Forest Service roads** (FSR #225), and there is a **Luna
  Park campground** — so this is near-certainly **US Forest Service**
  land, most likely the **Gila National Forest**. **The specific forest
  and ranger district were not confirmed.**
- **Easy camping** is called out as a feature of the area, presumably
  the campground.
- No closures, fees, permits or seasonal restrictions were recovered.
  Forest roads in this country are subject to fire closures and monsoon
  washouts; check current conditions.
