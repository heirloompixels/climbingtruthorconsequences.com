# Red Rock Arroyo

A Mountain Project area in the Truth or Consequences tree, north/west of
town in Forest Service country, sharing its road network with

## Location & Approach

This is the one part of the record that came through clearly. Two
approaches, from opposite ends:

**From the south:** via **Forest Service Road #139** from **Monticello,
New Mexico**.

**From the north:** off the **I-25 west frontage road**, approximately
**5 miles south of Exit 100 (Red Rock)**, onto Forest Service Road #139.

A further northern approach was described in the same source, though it
appears to be the approach to **Luna Park campground** rather than to
Red Rock Arroyo itself:

> From **Exit 115**, go **east** and follow **road #1** for about **11
> miles**, then continue **south** and then **west** on **Forest Service
> Road #225** to **Luna Park campground**.

> **Unverified.** The two areas' approaches were described together in
> the recovered text and may be conflated. **No GPS coordinates were
> captured**, and no approach-on-foot description, distance, or vehicle
> requirement was recovered. Read the area page before publishing any of
> this.

Monticello is a small village northwest of TorC; Exit 100 is south of
Exit 115 on I-25, so the two northern approaches come at the area from
different points.
