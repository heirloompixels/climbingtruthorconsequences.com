# Agent Log — research history

Every research subagent run on this project, with its reported
findings, kept as history. Method note common to all of the first
wave (2026-08-21): the environment's egress proxy blocks direct
fetching of Mountain Project, theCrag and most other sites, so all
findings are grounded in web-search snippets (≈200 searches per
agent), cross-checked where possible; unverifiable items are marked
`[unverified]` in the research files rather than guessed.

## Wave 1 — 2026-08-21

### 1. Tres Piedras, NM (featured) — completed
Wrote `research/featured/tres-piedras.md` (60+ source URLs). Solid:
geology (Tres Piedras Orthogneiss, ~1,693 Ma Precambrian pluton),
approach from US 64/285, ~8,000 ft elevation, formation names (North /
Middle / South / West / Far / Mosaic Rock), style (granite trad
slab/crack plus some sport, strong bouldering), Carson NF access with
a private-land corner and raptor closures, camping, and the two
guidebooks (Jay Foley, *Taos Rock*, 2005; Dennis Jackson, *Rock
Climbing New Mexico*). Thin: the moderates table — only three routes
with confirmed grades (Albuquerque Route 5.8 ★★ sport, Clean Green
Dream 5.9, Mama Jugs 5.8); other names surfaced without confirmable
grades (Zig Zag Man, South Face Right, Summer Dreams, Jug or Not,
MSMACITU) and are marked unconfirmed. No FA history surfaced.
Follow-up needed: direct Mountain Project pass or print guidebooks.

### 3. Colorado featured (Independence Pass, Penitente) — completed
Wrote `research/featured/independence-pass.md` and
`research/featured/penitente-canyon.md`. Independence Pass: current
guidebook is *The Pass: A Climber's Guide to Independence Pass and
Monitor Rock*, Jason Brown & Mike Schneiter (Wolverine, 2023, ~1,000
routes; predecessor Tom Perkins 2006). Monitor Rock (Big Rock)
confirmed ~60-route granite dome, West Face + East Side/Clairabell
Mine Wall; verified moderates: Twin Cracks (5.8, Lower Grotto Wall),
Left Cheek / Going Greek (5.9, Butt Wall), Home Wrecker / One Stick or
Two? (5.9, Clairabell Mine Wall), Guide Route (5.2–5.8 multi-pitch).
Summit 12,095 ft; seasonal CDOT closure confirmed. The suggested
route "Flight Deck" could NOT be verified — dropped. Penitente:
current guidebook *Rock Climbing the San Luis Valley*, Bob D'Antonio
(Falcon, 3rd ed. 1999); Fish Canyon Tuff / La Garita Caldera geology;
Virgin of Guadalupe mural history; BLM campground details. Famous
route is *Bullet the Blue Sky* (5.12c) — the brief's "Bullet the
Brown Cloud" was a name mix-up. Moderates skew thin because Penitente
genuinely skews 5.10+: The Serpent 5.7, May-B-Nueve 5.8, Tanks for
the Hueco 5.10d.

### 10. Design brief (guidebook patterns + Beardsley) — completed
Wrote `research/design-brief.md` (~13 sections; claims marked
[verified] vs [from familiarity] because the search budget died four
queries in). Information architecture: Region → Area → Crag → Route
with an optional Sector; type every node (`extra.kind` drives the
template); a node holds children or routes, never both. Routes as
their own pages would let Zola taxonomies generate `/grade/5.10/`,
`/style/trad/` filter pages with zero JavaScript. Route `weight`
sorts left-to-right as you face the rock; stars are editorial, not
voted. Adopt Mountain Project's Description/Location/Protection
headings verbatim. Highest-value component: the "condition strip"
(aspect · sun · season · approach · vehicle) under the crag title.
Beardsley: he drew for the photomechanical line block — pure black,
pure paper, no grey; the web is the same medium. Two accents with one
job each: Yellow Book ochre for ornament (<5% of any page), Caballo
red reserved for closures and R/X only. Three-tier ornament budget
(frontispiece spends everything; chapter pages one corner piece and a
divider; route/index pages stars and hairlines only) — the gradient
from ornate to bare IS the design. Ornament as inline SVG macros,
fill=currentColor, filled paths not strokes. Plates: numbered,
captioned, framed; an empty plate reads as a book awaiting its
engraving. Table numerals: `font-variant-numeric: lining-nums
tabular-nums`. Build order: tokens and macros first, then one fully
entered real crag before a second.
